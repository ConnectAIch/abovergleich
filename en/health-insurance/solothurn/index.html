Frequently asked questions

Which health insurer is cheapest in the canton of Solothurn in 2027?

For adults with a 2'500 deductible and accident cover, Agrisano (Telmed) is the cheapest at CHF 392.80 per month, across the whole canton. In the standard model with a 300 deductible it is Agrisano at CHF 582.40.

How much are health insurance premiums rising in 2027 in the canton of Solothurn?

The standard premium for adults with a 300 deductible rises by +5.7% on average in the canton of Solothurn, weighted by each insurer’s number of insured persons. Across Switzerland, the average premium rises by 5.0% according to the FOPH.

When is the deadline to switch health insurer for 2027?

Your cancellation of basic insurance must reach your insurer by 30 November 2026. The new insurer must accept you without health questions, and the switch takes effect on 1 January 2027.
