Frequently asked questions

How much are CSS premiums rising in 2027?

The standard premium for adults with a 300 deductible rises at CSS by +7.7% on average, weighted by insured persons per canton. According to the FOPH, the average premium of all insurers rises by 5.0%.

Is CSS cheap?

For adults without accident cover and a 2'500 deductible, CSS is among the three cheapest insurers in 1 of 26 cantons in 2027. How cheap it is for you depends on where you live, your deductible and the model.

When is the deadline to cancel CSS?

Your cancellation must reach CSS by 30 November 2026; you then switch on 1 January 2027. Address: CSS Kranken-Versicherung AG, Tribschenstrasse 21, Postfach 2568, 6002 Luzern.
