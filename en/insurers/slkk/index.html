Frequently asked questions

How much are SLKK premiums rising in 2027?

The standard premium for adults with a 300 deductible rises at SLKK by +3.6 % on average, weighted by insured persons per canton. According to the FOPH, the average premium of all insurers rises by 5.0%.

Is SLKK cheap?

For adults without accident cover and a 2'500 deductible, SLKK is among the three cheapest insurers in 1 of 21 cantons in 2027. How cheap it is for you depends on where you live, your deductible and the model.

When is the deadline to cancel SLKK?

Your cancellation must reach SLKK by 30 November 2026; you then switch on 1 January 2027. Address: Genossenschaft KRANKENKASSE SLKK, Hofwiesenstrasse 370, Postfach 5652, 8050 Zürich.
