Frequently asked questions

How much are Sanitas premiums rising in 2027?

The standard premium for adults with a 300 deductible rises at Sanitas by +3.3% on average, weighted by insured persons per canton. According to the FOPH, the average premium of all insurers rises by 5.0%.

Is Sanitas cheap?

For adults without accident cover and a 2'500 deductible, Sanitas is among the three cheapest insurers in 17 of 26 cantons in 2027 and the cheapest of all in 11 cantons. How cheap it is for you depends on where you live, your deductible and the model.

When is the deadline to cancel Sanitas?

Your cancellation must reach Sanitas by 30 November 2026; you then switch on 1 January 2027. Address: Sanitas Grundversicherungen AG, Jägergasse 3, Postfach 2010, 8021 Zürich.
