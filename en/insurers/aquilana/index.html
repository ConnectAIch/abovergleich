Frequently asked questions

How much are Aquilana premiums rising in 2027?

The standard premium for adults with a 300 deductible rises at Aquilana by +4.1 % on average, weighted by insured persons per canton. According to the FOPH, the average premium of all insurers rises by 5.0%.

Is Aquilana cheap?

For adults without accident cover and a 2'500 deductible, Aquilana is among the three cheapest insurers in 2 of 26 cantons in 2027. How cheap it is for you depends on where you live, your deductible and the model.

When is the deadline to cancel Aquilana?

Your cancellation must reach Aquilana by 30 November 2026; you then switch on 1 January 2027. Address: Aquilana Versicherungen, Bruggerstrasse 46, 5401 Baden.
