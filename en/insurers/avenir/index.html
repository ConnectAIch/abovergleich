Avenir models

Besides the standard model with free choice of doctor, Avenir offers these tariffs in 2027 (not every one in every canton). Benefits are the same in all models, only your first point of contact differs.

  • Alternative: PrimaFlex (flexibles Modell)
  • Family doctor: PrimaCare (Hausarzt)
  • HMO: OptiMed (Ärztenetz)
  • Telmed: SanaTel (Telemedizin)

Groupe Mutuel group

Avenir belongs to the Groupe Mutuel group. Other insurers in the group with their own premiums: Mutuel, Philos.

Frequently asked questions

How much are Avenir premiums rising in 2027?

The standard premium for adults with a 300 deductible rises at Avenir by +4.0 % on average, weighted by insured persons per canton. According to the FOPH, the average premium of all insurers rises by 5.0%.

Is Avenir cheap?

For adults without accident cover and a 2'500 deductible, Avenir is among the three cheapest insurers in 2 of 26 cantons in 2027. How cheap it is for you depends on where you live, your deductible and the model.

When is the deadline to cancel Avenir?

Your cancellation must reach Avenir by 30 November 2026; you then switch on 1 January 2027. Address: Avenir Krankenversicherung AG, Rue des Cèdres 5, 1919 Martigny.
