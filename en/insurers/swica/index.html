Swica models

Besides the standard model with free choice of doctor, Swica offers these tariffs in 2027 (not every one in every canton). Benefits are the same in all models, only your first point of contact differs.

  • Alternative: Favorit Medpharm, Favorit Multichoice
  • Family doctor: Favorit Casa, Favorit Medica
  • HMO: Favorit Bestcare, Favorit Santé
  • Telmed: Favorit Start, Favorit Telmed

Frequently asked questions

How much are Swica premiums rising in 2027?

The standard premium for adults with a 300 deductible rises at Swica by +3.6% on average, weighted by insured persons per canton. According to the FOPH, the average premium of all insurers rises by 5.0%.

Is Swica cheap?

For adults without accident cover and a 2'500 deductible, Swica is among the three cheapest insurers in 2 of 26 cantons in 2027 and the cheapest of all in 1 canton. How cheap it is for you depends on where you live, your deductible and the model.

When is the deadline to cancel Swica?

Your cancellation must reach Swica by 30 November 2026; you then switch on 1 January 2027. Address: SWICA Krankenversicherung AG, Römerstrasse 37, 8401 Winterthur.
