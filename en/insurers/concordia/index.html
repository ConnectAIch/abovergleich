Frequently asked questions

How much are Concordia premiums rising in 2027?

The standard premium for adults with a 300 deductible rises at Concordia by +2.8% on average, weighted by insured persons per canton. According to the FOPH, the average premium of all insurers rises by 5.0%.

Is Concordia cheap?

For adults without accident cover and a 2'500 deductible, Concordia is among the three cheapest insurers in 5 of 26 cantons in 2027 and the cheapest of all in 1 canton. How cheap it is for you depends on where you live, your deductible and the model.

When is the deadline to cancel Concordia?

Your cancellation must reach Concordia by 30 November 2026; you then switch on 1 January 2027. Address: CONCORDIA Schweiz. Kranken- und Unfallversicherung AG, Bundesplatz 15, 6002 Luzern.
