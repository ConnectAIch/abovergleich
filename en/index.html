Are the benefits really the same with every insurer?+
Yes. Basic health insurance (OKP) is regulated by law. Every insurer must provide exactly the same benefits, whether Assura, CSS or Helsana. The only difference is the price and the customer service.
When can I switch health insurer?+
Once a year, with effect from 1 January. Your cancellation must reach your current insurer by 30 November at the latest. The FOPH publishes the new premiums at the end of September each year. That is when comparing pays off.
What is the difference between standard, HMO and family doctor?+
Standard: free choice of doctor, highest premium.
Family doctor: you always see your family doctor first. Usually around 10% cheaper.
HMO: you go to a health centre. Usually around 12% cheaper.
Telmed: call first, then see a doctor. Usually around 10% cheaper, some tariffs up to 20%.
The medical benefits are the same in every model. Detailed guide →
Which deductible should I choose?+
Rule of thumb: if you rarely see a doctor, choose CHF 2'500 (lowest premium). If you need treatment regularly, choose CHF 300 (lowest cost sharing). With CHF 2'500 instead of CHF 300 you almost always pay CHF 1'540 less in premiums a year, around CHF 128 a month. The law does not allow a bigger difference. It pays off as long as your medical costs stay below about CHF 2'000 a year. Detailed guide →
Why do premiums vary so much depending on where you live?+
Switzerland is divided into premium regions. In urban areas (e.g. Zurich, Basel), healthcare costs are higher than in the countryside: more doctors, more specialists, more treatments. That is why premiums vary considerably by postcode.
