How we calculate

ComponentWeightWhat it measures
Price today30%Position of the insurer’s cheapest tariff among all insurers in the premium region, 2027. Among the cheapest 20% = 10 points, from 80% = 0.
Consistency25%In how many years since 2020 was the insurer among the 5 cheapest in the region? From 30% of years = 10 points.
Loyalty15%How much did the insurer’s cheapest tariff rise for those who stayed in it, compared with the median of all tariffs in the region? Average over all years. 0.3 points a year below market = 10, 1.5 points above = 0.
Discount retention10%Do new models keep their discount compared with the same insurer’s standard model? Measured on the same tariffs from launch year to today. No loss = 10, 2 points lost per year = 0. Insurers without new models since 2021 are not scored here.
Tariff continuity10%Share of tariffs that continue the following year under the same tariff code. 100% = 10 points, 80% = 0. Dropping or renaming tariffs forces customers to switch.
Reserves10%Solvency ratio according to the FOPH as of 1 January 2026: available reserves relative to the legal minimum. 200% = 10 points, 100% = 0. Thin reserves often come before larger increases.

We use the premiums of all insurers from 2020 to 2027 for adults, with and without accident cover and with deductibles of 300 and 2'500. This gives four separate scores; the overall score weights them by FOPH enrolment figures (With accident cover, deductible 300 24%, With accident cover, deductible 2'500 20%, Without accident cover, deductible 300 30%, Without accident cover, deductible 2'500 26%). Tariffs an insurer renames are followed by name. If a component is missing, its weight is spread over the others. The formula is the same for every insurer, and no insurer pays us anything.

Frequently asked questions

Which health insurer is consistently cheap?

According to our Price Consistency Rating 2027, Concordia, Agrisano and KPT come out best. They are cheap today and were in previous years too. Your canton’s page shows which insurer leads in your region.

Why is today’s cheapest insurer often not the best choice?

Some insurers are cheap for one year and then raise prices more than average. New savings models start with a big discount and lose it in the following years. If you don’t want to switch every year, a consistently cheap insurer serves you better.

Do customer reviews count in the rating?

No. The rating relies only on hard figures from the Federal Office of Public Health: premiums since 2020, solvency ratios and supervisory data. Every number can be recalculated.

Do insurers pay for a good score?

No. abovergleich.com takes no commissions from health insurers. The score comes from a fixed formula, published here.
