    How the switch works

    1. Send the cancellation: by email if your insurer accepts that (shown with the letter), otherwise by post. Received by 30 November 2026; the postmark does not count.
    2. Sign up with your new insurer for 1 January 2027. Online in about 10 minutes, no health questions. It must accept you.
    3. Wait for confirmation. The new insurer tells the old one that you are insured with it. Until then you stay insured with the old one, never without cover.

    Important: if you still have unpaid premiums or cost-sharing with your current insurer on 31 December, you cannot switch. Pay any open bills first.

    Special cases

    • Only changing model or deductible with the same insurer: also possible from 1 January. The deadline is in your insurer’s terms; you are safe if you notify them by 30 November 2026.
    • Cancelling for the end of June: only in the standard model with a 300 deductible, received by 31 March.
    • Moving abroad or death: the insurance ends without notice on the day you leave or the day of death.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is the deadline to cancel my health insurance?

    Your cancellation of basic insurance must reach the insurer by 30 November 2026. What counts is the date the insurer receives the letter, not the postmark. The switch takes effect on 1 January 2027.

    Do I have to cancel by registered mail?

    No. The law prescribes no form; what matters is that the cancellation arrives on time. According to their own websites, 11 of 32 insurers also accept it by email; the table below shows which. By post, registered mail is the safest proof; by email, the insurer’s confirmation of receipt.

    Can the new health insurer refuse me?

    No. In basic insurance, every insurer must accept everyone in its area of operation, without health questions. Supplementary insurance is different.

    What happens to my supplementary insurance?

    Nothing, unless you cancel it yourself. Supplementary insurance can stay with your current insurer even if you switch basic insurance. It has its own notice periods in the contract. Switching it rarely pays off: the new insurer may ask health questions and impose exclusions. Advisers still often recommend it, because they earn a commission.

    Can I also switch at the end of June?

    Only in the standard model with a 300 deductible. The cancellation must then arrive by 31 March, and the switch takes effect on 1 July.
