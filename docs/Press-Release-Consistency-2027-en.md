# Swiss health insurance 2027: don’t just pick the cheapest, pick the most consistent
### A new rating based on eight years of federal premium data shows which insurers stay cheap over time

**Zurich, October 2026.** Switching to the cheapest health insurer every year often backfires. The independent comparison site abovergleich.com analysed the premiums of every Swiss insurer from 2020 to 2027 in every premium region, using only data from the Federal Office of Public Health (FOPH). The result: new savings models start with a large discount and lose it year after year, and last year’s cheapest insurer raises its prices more than the market. If you don’t want to switch every year, a **price-consistent insurer** is the better choice. Nationally, **Concordia, Agrisano and KPT** come out on top. In the cantons with the most international residents, the most consistent insurers are **KPT in Zurich, Concordia in Zug and Basel-Stadt, ÖKK in Basel-Landschaft and Sanitas in Geneva and Vaud.**

---

## Key findings

**1. New savings models lose their discount.**
As long as an insurer has no five years of cost data, a new model may start up to 20% below the standard model (Art. 101 of the Health Insurance Ordinance, KVV). After that, the discount has to be justified by real costs. The data show what that means: the savings models launched in 2021 were **18.4%** cheaper than the same insurer’s standard model; today the gap is only **13.2%** (deductible 2'500, same tariffs tracked).

**2. Last year’s cheapest insurer rises more.**
The cheapest tariff of 2026 rises by **7.7%** on average in 2027, the market by 5.4%. It is still the cheapest in only 8 of 36 regions. The year before was the same. Examples, for adults with a 2'500 deductible and no accident cover:

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Zug | Concordia, HMO | CHF 196.00 → 215.00 | +9.7% | +5.9% | 2 |
| Basel-Stadt | CSS, family doctor | CHF 416.30 → 446.50 | +7.3% | +4.8% | 4 |
| Geneva | Assura, family doctor (PharMed) | CHF 442.70 → 466.20 | +5.3% | +3.2% | 3 |
| Vaud, region 2 | Assura, family doctor (PharMed) | CHF 380.40 → 402.70 | +5.9% | +3.1% | 2 |

All figures can be checked on priminfo.admin.ch.

**3. Some insurers stay cheap over time.**
The abovergleich.com Price Consistency Rating gives every insurer a score from 0 to 10: price today, how often it has been among the five cheapest in its region since 2020, how much it raises prices, whether new models keep their discount, how often it drops tariffs, and how strong its reserves are. Nationally, **Concordia (9.1), Agrisano (8.6) and KPT (8.4)** lead. Because insurers price accident cover and deductibles differently, the score exists for each situation: with or without accident cover, deductible 300 or 2'500. The overall score weights them by how many adults choose each option.

**Price Consistency Award 2027: the most consistent insurer in every canton.**
The rating is the basis for the Price Consistency Award: Concordia wins overall ahead of Agrisano and KPT, with categories such as “Consistently cheap” (Assura) and “Most stable premiums” (ÖKK), and a winner in every canton:

## What to do: advice by situation

| Situation | Advice |
|---|---|
| **You don’t want to switch every year** | Choose an insurer with a high price consistency score in your region. The abovergleich.com calculator flags offers that have almost always been among the cheapest since 2020 as “consistently cheap”. |
| **You are in the standard model** | First switch model with your current insurer (family doctor, HMO, Telmed). Benefits are identical by law; the median saving is **CHF 871 a year**, with no cancellation needed. |
| **You switched to the cheapest insurer last year** | Compare again this year: last year’s cheapest insurers rise more than average. |
| **You are 25** | From the year you turn 26, the adult premium applies, a median **48%** more in the same tariff (deductible 2'500). Compare again before you move into the adult age group. |
| **You want to cancel** | Your cancellation must **reach** the insurer by **30 November**; the postmark does not count. According to their own websites, 11 insurers covering 58% of insured persons also accept cancellation by email. The abovergleich.com letter tool creates the cancellation letter in German or French, with the right address and your signature, and emails it to you as a PDF in two minutes. |

## Methodology

The basis is the premiums of all Swiss health insurers from 2020 to 2027 for every premium region, published by the FOPH (opendata.swiss), plus the solvency ratios published by the FOPH as of 1 January 2026. We analysed adults with and without accident cover, with deductibles of 300 and 2'500, weighted by the number of insured persons according to the FOPH. The rating has six components: price today (30%), consistency (25%), loyalty (15%), discount retention of new models (10%), tariff continuity (10%) and reserves (10%). The formula is published on the website. Customer reviews play no part, and no insurer pays for its ranking.
